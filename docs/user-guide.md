*📺 THE OFFICE DISPLAY — HOW TO USE IT*

Everything on the screen is updated *by email*. No app, no login.

Send to: *your-dashboard@gmail.com*
⚠️ You must send from your *@yourteam.dev* address, or it will be ignored.

Changes show up on the screen within about *5–10 minutes*.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*🖼️ TO ADD PHOTOS*
Attach the photos and send. Subject can be anything.
• Up to 10 photos per email
• They join the slideshow (one every 45 seconds)
• The screen keeps the 40 most recent

*📢 TO PUT A MESSAGE ON THE BOTTOM BANNER*
Subject: `notice: Coffee machine is fixed`
That's it — the text after "notice:" becomes the banner.

*🧹 TO CLEAR THE BANNER*
Subject: `notice:`  (nothing after it, empty email)

*🎨 TO CHANGE THE BANNER COLOUR*
Send a notice as above, and put this on a line in the message body:
`#accent=#E8A33D`

*📌 TO HOLD ONE PHOTO ON SCREEN*
Attach the photo and use subject: `pinphoto:`
It gets added and held on screen straight away — no slideshow.

To hold a photo that's *already* on the screen, put part of its name after
`pinphoto:` — or just walk over and *click the photo on the screen itself*.

*▶️ TO GO BACK TO THE SLIDESHOW*
Subject: `pinphoto: off`  (no attachment)
Or click the photo on the screen again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*🎉 WHEN SOMETHING NEW ARRIVES*
When your photos or your notice reach the screen, it throws a short burst
of confetti with *NEW PHOTOS* or *NEW NOTICE* across the middle. It clears
itself after a few seconds — there's nothing to dismiss.

That's your confirmation the email got through. If you don't see it within
10 minutes, the email didn't land (check you sent from your @yourteam.dev
address).

It only fires for things that are genuinely new, so restarting the screen
doesn't set it off, and neither does clearing the banner.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*📅 THE CALENDAR*
Updates itself from the team Google Calendar every 15 minutes.
Nothing to email — just add your event to the calendar as normal.

*📇 THE DIRECTORY*
The contacts column on the right *scrolls* — drag it up and down with the
mouse (or your finger) to see everyone. A thin scrollbar appears on the
right of the column when there's more below.

*Click* the column to open the full-page list instead, which shows job
titles too. Click *Back* to return.

Names and numbers come from a *Google Sheet*, not from email.
Ask the office manager to add or correct an entry — the screen
picks up the change within about 15 minutes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*🌙 SCREEN BRIGHTNESS & OFF-HOURS*

Out of the box the screen is bright *8am–6pm*, dimmed *6pm–10pm*, and
*off 10pm–8am*.

To change those times, tap the small *⚙️ gear* in the bottom-right corner
of the screen (a long press works too). You get three settings:

• *Bright from* — when the screen wakes up in the morning
• *Dim at* — when it fades down for the evening
• *Screen off at* — when the panel switches off for the night
• *Evening dimness* — how dark the evening fade is (slider)

The times must run in order: bright → dim → off. If they don't, *Save*
won't light up.

*These settings are saved.* Once you press *Save* they stay put — through
a reboot, a power cut, or someone unplugging the screen at the wall.
You only need to set them once.

*Reset* puts the original times back (8am / 6pm / 10pm).

*💤 WAKING THE SCREEN AT NIGHT*
Tap anywhere on a dark screen and it lights up for *1 minute*, then goes
back to sleep on its own.
