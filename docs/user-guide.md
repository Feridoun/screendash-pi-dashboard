*📺 THE OFFICE DISPLAY — HOW TO USE IT*

Everything on the screen is updated *by email* — except the *DOCTORS
ROTA*, which has its own form (see below). No app, no login.

Send to: *your-dashboard@gmail.com*
⚠️ You must send from your *@yourteam.dev* address, or it will be ignored.

Changes show up on the screen within about *5–10 minutes*.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*✉️ HOW AN EMAIL IS READ*
The *subject* says what to do — `notice`, `message`, `pinphoto`, `delete` —
and the *body* is what you're saying. Nothing else needs to go in the subject.

*🖼️ TO ADD PHOTOS*
Attach the photos and send. Subject can be anything (except those four words).
• Up to 10 photos per email
• They join the slideshow (one every 45 seconds)
• The screen keeps the 60 most recent

Small images are ignored on purpose — that's how signature logos and other
email decoration are kept off the wall.

*📢 TO PUT A MESSAGE ON THE BOTTOM BANNER*
Subject: `notice`
Body: `Coffee machine is fixed`
That's it — what you write in the email becomes the banner. Only the first
paragraph is used: anything after the first blank line is dropped, which is
how your signature is kept off the wall. A notice can run to several lines,
just don't leave an empty line in the middle of it.

*🧹 TO CLEAR THE BANNER*
Subject: `notice` with an *empty* email

*🎨 TO CHANGE THE BANNER COLOUR*
Send a notice as above, and put this on a line in the message body:
`#accent=#E8A33D`

*💬 TO POST IN THE MESSAGES PANEL*
Subject: `message`
Body: `Cakes in the kitchen, help yourselves`
It appears in the *MESSAGES* box under the calendar, with your name in
front of it. Unlike the banner, these stack up — newest at the top, the
last 50 are kept, and nothing needs clearing.

Use the banner for "everyone needs to know this"; use messages for
everything else.

*📌 TO HOLD ONE PHOTO ON SCREEN*
Attach the photo and use subject: `pinphoto`
It gets added and held on screen straight away — no slideshow.

To hold a photo that's *already* on the screen, put part of its name in the
body — or just walk over and use the *pin button* on the screen itself (see
below).

*▶️ TO GO BACK TO THE SLIDESHOW*
Subject: `pinphoto`, no attachment, body `off` (or empty)
Or press the pin button on the screen again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*🗑️ TO TAKE A PHOTO BACK DOWN*

Photos are identified by *the email that added them*, because the filenames
are made up by the system and never shown anywhere.

So: open your *Sent* folder, find the email you sent the photos in, and
*reply* to it with the subject `delete`

Three things to get right:
• the reply must go to *your-dashboard@gmail.com* (check the To line —
  replying to your own sent mail sometimes puts your own address there)
• the subject must be `delete` — leaving *Re:* on the front is fine,
  `Re: delete` works
• don't worry about the quoted photos underneath; attachments on a
  `delete` email are ignored, so nothing gets re-added

*That removes every photo that email added.*

*🔢 TO REMOVE JUST ONE OF THEM*
Subject: `delete`, body: `2`
Removes only the *second* photo of that email, counting in the order you
attached them. A body of `1`, `3` and so on works the same way. Anything
chattier than a bare number or a filename is ignored, so a stray "thanks"
still removes the lot rather than nothing.

*🧯 IF THE ORIGINAL EMAIL IS LONG GONE*
Ask the office manager — they can remove a photo by name from the mailbox
end. From the screen itself you can also hide it locally (see below).

*Good to know*
• Nothing is really destroyed. Removed photos are kept in the archive, so
  a mistake can be undone — ask the office manager.
• A delete removes photos only. To clear the banner use `notice`, and
  messages fall off the panel on their own.
• If the reply doesn't trace back to any photos, nothing happens — the
  screen just carries on. No confirmation is sent, so give it 10 minutes
  and look at the wall.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*👆 WHAT YOU CAN DO AT THE SCREEN ITSELF*

Touch the photo and a row of controls fades in along the bottom. They fade
out again a few seconds after you stop touching them.

• *◀ ▶* — step through the photos by hand
• *Fill / Fit / Zoom* — tapping the photo itself cycles how it's cropped
  into the panel. Handy for a group shot with someone's head cut off.
  It only affects the photo on screen, and only until it moves on.
• *📌 pin* — hold this photo on screen. Press again to let the slideshow
  carry on. The pin badge stays visible while it's pinned, so nobody
  wonders why the wall stopped changing.
• *🗑️ bin (tap twice to confirm)* — hides that photo *on this screen only*.
  It is not deleted: other screens and the mailbox are untouched. Use the
  `delete` email above if you want it gone for everyone.

The local hide sticks through a reboot and can't be undone at the screen —
if you hid one by accident, have it deleted and re-sent by email.

*📢 THE BANNER* has arrows too. Touch it and use *◀ ▶* to step back
through recent notices, for anyone who was away when the last one went up.
It returns to the current notice by itself after about half a minute.

*🔄 THE REFRESH DOT* (bottom-right, next to the gear) pulls everything in
now instead of waiting for the next check — useful straight after sending
an email. It's also the connection light: *green* is healthy, *amber-red*
means the screen can't reach the internet.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*🎉 WHEN SOMETHING NEW ARRIVES*
When your photos or your notice reach the screen, it throws a short burst
of confetti with *NEW PHOTOS* or *NEW NOTICE* across the middle. It clears
itself after a few seconds — there's nothing to dismiss.

That's your confirmation the email got through. If you don't see it within
10 minutes, the email didn't land (check you sent from your @yourteam.dev
address).

It only fires for things that are genuinely new, so restarting the screen
doesn't set it off, and neither does clearing the banner, posting a
message, or deleting a photo.

*📅 THE CALENDAR*
Updates itself from the team Google Calendar every 15 minutes.
Nothing to email — just add your event to the calendar as normal.

*🩺 THE DOCTORS ROTA — WHO'S IN TODAY*

Under the directory, bottom right, there's a table with every doctor on
the team and what they're doing *today and the next two working days* —
a column per day, dated across the top. Nothing to email. It's built
from two things:

• *your usual week* — the days and hours you normally work, and any
  regular day that isn't a ward day (a WFH day, a clinic day)
• *your exceptions* — leave, study days, sick days, a meeting you'll be
  out for, a one-off day working from home

So you set your usual week *once*, and then only tell it about the
exceptions, as they come up.

*🔴🟢 READING THE ROTA*
The dot next to each name says the whole story from across the room:

• 🟢 *green* — in, as usual. The hours are beside it (`8–6`)
• 🟡 *amber* — working, but not on the ward for some or all of the day.
  Read the words: `Meeting 10–12 · phone` means in as usual except
  10–12, and reachable by phone during it. `Remote · email` means at
  home all day. `WFH` or `Clinic` with no hours is a regular day of
  that from your usual week
• 🔴 *red* — not working today when they normally would be:
  `Annual leave`, `Study leave`, `Sick`. After the dot you'll see how
  to reach them, if at all: `· phone`, `· email`, or `· none`
• ⚪ *hollow* — not one of their days. Just `Off`, nothing to read into it

Everyone on the team is always on the rota, so if a name is missing
the rota sheet needs updating — see *your usual week* below.

Weekend days are skipped unless someone actually works them, so on a
Friday the columns are *Fri · Mon · Tue*, and at the weekend it shows
the coming week. The date at the top of each column says which day it
is; today's is the brighter one.

*📋 YOUR USUAL WEEK*
This lives on the *Team* tab of the rota spreadsheet — one row per
doctor, a cell for each weekday with your hours in it, blank for the
days you don't work. Something like:

  Dr A Khan · Consultant · Mon `08:00-18:00` · Tue — · Wed — ·
  Thu `08:00-18:00` · Fri `08:00-18:00`

Ask the office manager to set yours up, or edit it yourself if you've
been given the link. Three tips if you're editing:

• write hours with a colon or am/pm — `08:00-18:00` or `8am-6pm`.
  Google Sheets turns a plain `8-6` into a *date* (8th June!) and the
  screen will show you at default hours
• a regular day that isn't a ward day can just say what it is —
  `WFH`, `Clinic`, `Community` — instead of hours, and the screen
  shows that word, in amber, every week. Add hours if they matter:
  `Clinic 10:00-12:00`. Leave booked over such a day replaces it
• `AM` or `PM` in a cell is a half day; `y` means "the usual hours"
• leave a day *blank* (or put a dash) if you don't work it

*Rotations:* the *From* and *Until* columns at the end of your row
are for start and finish dates. New doctors can be typed in before they
arrive and appear on the card on their first day; the outgoing row
drops off after its *Until* date. Nobody has to remember to edit
anything on changeover day.

The names in the leave form follow the *Team* tab by themselves,
within about 15 minutes. A new doctor can book leave as soon as their
row is in, and a doctor who has left stops appearing after their
*Until* date. Don't edit the form's name list: the Team tab overwrites it.

An *Email* column on the Team tab is optional. Fill it in if you want
people told when their leave leaves the ward short (see below).

*Who gets the emails* about bookings and short-staffed days is the
*Notify_list* tab of the same spreadsheet: one address per cell, in
any row. Add or remove someone there and it applies from the next
sync, about 15 minutes.

*🏖️ BOOKING LEAVE, STUDY, SICK, A MEETING, WFH*
Use the form — it takes about thirty seconds on a phone and needs
no login:

  *📝 <rota form link — ask the office manager>*

You'll be asked:

• *Name* — pick yourself from the list
• *First day* and *Last day* — leave *Last day* blank for a single day
• *Type* — Annual leave, Study leave, Sick, Meeting, Working from
  home, Off, or Other (say what in the note)
• *Hours* — *leave this blank if it's the whole day.* Only fill it in
  for something inside a working day, e.g. `10-12` for a meeting.
  Then the card shows you as *in*, with the meeting flagged in amber
• *Contact* — tick how you can be reached: Phone, Email, both, or
  nothing ticked for "not contactable"
• *Note* — optional; a word or two, e.g. `Trust board`, `MRCPsych`

That's it. Every submission is also emailed to the people who arrange
cover, so you don't need to tell them separately. Submit, and the screen catches up within about 15 minutes —
or straight away if someone presses the *refresh dot* on the screen.

A few examples of how it comes out:

• On leave Mon–Fri next week, phone off →
  Name: you · First: Mon · Last: Fri · Type: Annual leave ·
  Contact: nothing ticked
  → 🔴 `Annual leave · none` all week
• Study day Thursday, checking email →
  First: Thu · Type: Study leave · Contact: Email
  → 🔴 `Study leave · email`
• Trust board 10–12 on Tuesday, ring if urgent →
  First: Tue · Type: Meeting · Hours: `10-12` · Contact: Phone
  → 🟡 `Meeting 10–12 · phone`
• Working from home Friday →
  First: Fri · Type: Working from home · Contact: Phone, Email
  → 🟡 `Remote · phone or email`
• Called in sick this morning →
  First: today · Type: Sick
  → 🔴 `Sick` (someone can submit this for you)

*↩️ CANCELLING OR CHANGING*
Don't go looking for the old entry. Just submit the form again:

• *to cancel* — same dates, Type *Cancel*. Your usual week is back
• *to change* — same dates, the new details. The newest entry wins

If you've booked leave that starts on a non-working day of yours, don't
worry: the card shows the leave right through, not `Off` on your
day off and `Annual leave` either side.

*💡 GOOD TO KNOW*
• Leave can be booked as far ahead as you like; the screen only shows
  the next three working days, but the rest is kept
• Your name isn't in the form's list? You're not on the *Team* tab
  yet — ask the office manager
• If your leave means *more than three doctors* (not counting
  consultants) are away for the whole day, you'll get an email, and so
  will the people who arrange cover. Your leave is still booked. The
  email is so cover can be sorted out, or the leave moved
• There's no confirmation. Look at the screen after 15 minutes, or ask
  someone to press the refresh dot
• If the card ever says *Rota not available*, the sync has stopped —
  tell the office manager. It says that rather than show yesterday's
  roster as today's

*🌦️ THE WEATHER*
Today and tomorrow, beside the clock — high and low, the conditions, and
the chance of rain when it's worth mentioning. Fully automatic; there's
nothing to send and nothing to set. If the strip is missing, the forecast
simply hasn't come through — it stays blank rather than showing you
yesterday's.

*📇 THE DIRECTORY*
The contacts list, top right, *scrolls* — drag it up and down with the
mouse (or your finger) to see everyone. A thin scrollbar appears on the
right when there's more below. It eases back to the top on its own once
you've walked away.

*Click* it (the *Scroll, or click to expand* line underneath is a good
target) to open the full-page list instead, which shows job titles too.
Click *Back* to return.

Names and numbers come from a *Google Sheet*, not from email.
Ask the office manager to add or correct an entry — the screen
picks up the change within about half an hour, or straight away if
someone presses the refresh dot.

*🌙 SCREEN BRIGHTNESS & OFF-HOURS*

Out of the box the screen is bright *8am–6pm*, dimmed *6pm–10pm*, and
*off 10pm–8am*.

To change those times, tap the small *⚙️ gear* in the bottom-right corner
of the screen (a long press works too). You get three settings:

• *Bright from* — when the screen wakes up in the morning
• *Dim at* — when it fades down for the evening
• *Screen off at* — when the panel switches off for the night
• *Evening dimness* — how dark the evening fade is (slider)

The times must run in order: bright → dim → off. If they don't, *Save*
won't light up.

*These settings are saved.* Once you press *Save* they stay put — through
a reboot, a power cut, or someone unplugging the screen at the wall.
You only need to set them once.

*Reset* puts the original times back (8am / 6pm / 10pm).

*💤 WAKING THE SCREEN AT NIGHT*
Tap anywhere on a dark screen and it lights up for *1 minute*, then goes
back to sleep on its own.
